# Idea Launch engine

Turns an idea memo into a verified, tiered campaign list, as Claude commands. One Google Sheet and one run folder per idea. Plan and decisions: `PLAN.md` (in docs/). What was reused from where: `REUSE-MAP.md`. Third-party notice: `NOTICE.md`.

**Status: all three phases built, plus the 2026-10-22 additions (company signals, two pilot gates, employment exclusions; see "Phase 4 design" below).** Phase 3: `/iv-copy`, `/iv-idea-page`, `/iv-launch` and the orchestrator `/iv-run`. Phase 3 was tested on the TEST run (see "Phase 3 test run" at the end).
Earlier: Phase 1 and Phase 2 built. Phase 1: `/iv-brief`, `/iv-accounts`, `/iv-qualify`. Phase 2: `/iv-people`, `/iv-tier`, `/iv-enrich-verify`, `/iv-activity`, `/iv-phones`, `/iv-ready` (this replaces the plan's single `/iv-verify`). Phase 2 was tested end to end on the TEST run (see "Phase 2 test run" at the end).

## Run it (any team member)
Open Claude Code in the Blissgrowth folder and run, in this order:

| Command | What it does | You do |
|---|---|---|
| `/iv-brief` | Reads the memo and ICP doc (Notion, Google Doc, PDF or pasted), interviews you (including which company signals matter and their thresholds), saves `brief.json`, creates the run folder and the Google Sheet | Answer one question at a time, confirm the brief |
| `/iv-accounts <idea>` | PILOT FIRST: pulls 10 to 20 accounts (default 15), enriches their signals, qualifies them, then STOPS for your hand review; after your explicit approval the full Apollo pull runs into the "2 Accounts" tab | Mark each pilot account approve or reject, approve the pilot |
| `/iv-signals <idea>` | Apollo organization enrichment by domain: employees and department headcounts (engineering, operations, sales ...) into "2 Accounts" (1 credit per matched company, once per company) | Say go (pilot accounts first) |
| `/iv-qualify <idea>` | Tunes a judge prompt on samples of 10 with you, then scores every company with Claude subagents; hard signal thresholds drop companies automatically, soft ones inform the judge | Mark wrong rows until 2 clean rounds |
| `/iv-people <idea>` | Apollo people search, one company per call, T1 and T2 titles together (0 credits). No-people companies go to "3 Accounts: No People" | Say go |
| `/iv-tier <idea>` | Claude tiers people T1 / T2 / X from the brief's tier rules, tuned on samples of 10 with you, then caps are enforced; then a PEOPLE PILOT of 10 to 20 is reviewed by hand before any enrichment credit | Review samples until 2 clean rounds, mark the pilot people, approve |
| `/iv-enrich-verify <idea>` | Apollo bulk_match for the capped people (credits; blocked until the people pilot is approved), MillionVerifier, waterfall list, list quality grade; employment exclusions start here | Confirm the credit estimate |
| `/iv-activity <idea>` | LinkedIn activity check via the n8n checker (on for the run only); its headline and job columns feed the employment exclusions | Say go |
| `/iv-phones <idea>` | T1 phones via Apollo's async reveal and an n8n receiver (8 credits per mobile) | Confirm the estimate |
| `/iv-ready <idea>` | Channels per person and the three Ready tabs | Check the counts |
| `/iv-copy <idea>` | Drafts LinkedIn invite, follow-ups (Jay or Meelan) and InMail (founder's office associate), V1 plus variants per tier, rule and spam check, tuning on samples, writes "8 Copy" and `copy.json` | Review the samples, approve explicitly |
| `/iv-idea-page <idea>` | Creates the idea's page in Notion "Idea Contexts" (Blissgrowth) for the reply agent, with the Notion MCP | Confirm the text |
| `/iv-launch <idea>` | HeyReach lists and DRAFT campaigns (never started) with the approved copy, plus Mansi's call sheet | Approve the plan |
| `/iv-run <idea>` | Runs every stage in order, resumable, stops only where you must decide (including both pilot gates) | Decide at the stops |

`<idea>` is the slug, the folder name, or omit it to pick from `python3 -I engine/bin/run_status.py --list`. Re-running any command resumes; it never re-pays for work already done.

## Stages (registry)
Stages are registered in `lib/stages.py` (name, how completion is derived from files, next command): brief, pilot_accounts, accounts, signals, qualify, people, tier, pilot_people, enrich_verify, activity, phones, ready, copy, idea_page, launch. `launch` is registered `post_ready`: it is the action AFTER READY, so READY means "everything up to and including copy and the idea page is done" and the next command is then `/iv-launch`. `summary.md` line 1 is READY only when every stage is `done` or `skipped` (a skip is a recorded user decision: `tier/skipped.json`, `activity/skipped.json`, `phones/skipped.json`) and the run is not a TEST. To add a stage write a check function and call `stages.register()`; nothing else changes.

## Phase 2 design decisions
- **People search**: one company per call (`organization_ids`, the domain as fallback) because Apollo returns no org id per person; all T1 and T2 titles in the same call, seniorities are the union of both lists (Apollo ANDs titles with seniorities). A per-company ledger makes it resumable; the client stops cleanly when the hourly budget is nearly used (200 per minute, 6,000 per hour on this key).
- **Caps**: one helper, `brief.people_caps(brief)`: `brief.people.caps` overrides `config.json` `people_caps` (T1 5, T2 3). Caps are applied AFTER tiering. Before tiering, title priority (position in the brief's title lists, T1 titles first) only bounds the tiering pool (`people.tier_pool_per_company`, 10) and, inside a tier, decides who stays under the cap. If tiering is skipped (user decision), the top T1 cap + T2 cap people by title priority are selected, tier stays blank, they get the T2 channels. Over-cap people stay in the sheet as `over_cap`.
- **Enrichment is mandatory**, search gives an obfuscated last name and no LinkedIn URL or email. Only `selected` people are enriched (T1 first), 10 per `people/bulk_match` call, real credits from `credits_consumed`. Apollo charged about 1 credit per person even when no email came back (measured: 15 people, 15 credits, 9 emails). Placeholder emails (`email_not_unlocked@domain.com`) are treated as missing. After enrichment, duplicates by LinkedIn URL or email are marked `duplicate`.
- **Email**: only MillionVerifier `ok` passes. `catch_all` is kept and marked (`waterfall_needed` = `catch_all_secondary_verify`), `unknown`, `invalid`, `disposable` fail, role addresses (info@) are not sendable, `error` is retried. Verdicts: valid, catch_all, role, invalid, unknown, error, missing, unverified. Cache per email in `verify/mv.jsonl`. The single API is used (browser User-Agent, 5 workers); the bulk file API is not needed at 2,000 to 3,000 emails. `MILLIONVERIFIER_API_KEY` goes in the project `.env`; without it verification is skipped with a clear message.
- **Waterfall (hooks only)**: Prospeo and Amplemarket are not set up. `people.csv` column `waterfall_needed` and `enrich/waterfall-needed.csv` list who needs a second source; `lib/waterfall.py` has the provider interface and raises "not set up". An Amplemarket key exists in `.env` and is deliberately unused.
- **List quality**: `lib/quality.py` ports the 8 checks of coldoutboundskills list-quality-scorecard (MIT) with three marked adaptations (domain limit = T1 cap + T2 cap, ICP fit = qualify verdict because we do not filter industry or headcount, MV catch-all added to role addresses). Grade in `summary.md`, Run Log and `list-quality.md`. Under 100 rows it is indicative only.
- **Channels**: T1 LinkedIn + email + call, T2 LinkedIn + email; LinkedIn needs `linkedin_active` yes, email needs verdict valid, call needs T1 and a phone. Ready tabs are upserted by `person_id` (first column), rows are never deleted, a person who stops qualifying becomes `withdrawn`.

## LinkedIn activity contract
The checker is the n8n workflow "LinkedIn Activity Checker" (id `NrweLKOV7gMu54bD`, name and id unchanged, normally INACTIVE; builder `engine/vendor/activity-checker/build_workflow_v21.py`). One small patch (`workflows/patch_activity_checker.py`, marker `IL_CONTRACT_V1`, backed up first, idempotent, also in the builder) lets a caller send `sheet_id`, `leads_tab`, `runs_tab` in the webhook body for one run (validated; defaults stay the Config node values), so no rebuild is needed per run.
- **Input**: profile URLs (`https://www.linkedin.com/in/<slug>`) plus optional name, title, company, upserted with `{"action":"upsert","rows":[...]}`; run started with `{"action":"run","max_leads":N}`.
- **Output** per URL (columns in the leads tab): `activity_status` (Active, Inactive, Shell, blank), `active_via`, `activity_reason`, `activity_checked_at`, `scrape_error`, `scrape_attempts`. Run cost in the runs tab (`usage_usd`).
- **Flow**: back up, activate, upsert, wait for rows, run, poll the runs tab for `run_end`, read the leads tab through the Sheets Bridge, cache final answers, deactivate (also after errors; after a timeout it stays on and `--resume` collects and then switches it off). A blank status with fewer than 3 attempts is not final and is retried on the next run.
- **Cache**: `activity/cache.jsonl` by normalised URL, a profile is never checked twice. Tabs: `il <run name>` and `il <run name> runs` in the checker's own spreadsheet (config `activity.sheet_id`), as earlier campaigns used a tab pair each.
- **Mapping**: Active yes, Inactive and Shell no, blank after the last attempt unknown (not active unless `activity.unknown_policy` is `include`).

## Apollo phones (async) and the receiver
Phone reveal is asynchronous: `people/bulk_match` with `reveal_phone_number=true` and a `webhook_url` answers at once with `phone_enrichment {status, request_id}`; Apollo POSTs the numbers later to the n8n workflow **"Idea Launch | Apollo Phone Webhook"** (deploy once with `workflows/deploy_phone_webhook.py`; permanent and ACTIVE, stores every POST as rows of the n8n data table **"IL Phone Results"** keyed by `person_id`, with `request_id`, `phones_json`, `raw_json`, `received_at`). `lib/phones.py` polls the table through the n8n API and records `requested` then `received` / `none` / `not_available` per person in `phones/phones.jsonl`. T1 only; 8 credits per mobile found; nobody is requested twice. The webhook path carries a random secret (Apollo cannot send headers) saved in `config.local.json` under `phones`. Alternative that needs no receiver: `poll_only=true` and `GET /webhook_result/{request_id}` (`ApolloClient.webhook_result`).

## Sheets Bridge additions (Phase 2)
`add_columns {sheet_id, tab, headers[]}` appends missing headers to row 1 of an existing tab (idempotent, widens the grid, bold header; `SheetClient.ensure_schema` migrates a whole sheet to `lib/schema.py`; every Phase 2 push does it first). `share {sheet_id, domain?, emails?, role?}` uses the Drive API permissions endpoint with the same Google credential: it works because the n8n credential carries the `drive.file` scope, which covers files it created (the idea sheets). New sheets are shared automatically on creation with `sheet.share` from `config.json` (the whole blissgrowth.com domain can edit; add emails in `config.local.json`; `auto_on_create` false to turn off; a failed share only prints a warning). Manual: `bin/sheet_tool.py share <sheet_id> [--domain d] [--email a@b.c]`. If Google refuses (scope, admin policy), the error says to share by hand. Every deploy of the bridge saves a backup in `workflows/backups/` first.

## Change behaviour without touching code
- **Defaults**: `config.json` (people caps T1 5 / T2 3, target 2,000 to 3,000 emails and 800 LinkedIn, volume-math rates, batch sizes, confidence threshold, clean rounds needed, deadlines). Personal overrides and the bridge secret: `config.local.json` (gitignored).
- **Volume math** (how many accounts to pull): `volume_assumptions` in `config.json`, or per brief under `volume.assumptions`. Replace the guesses with measured rates after a real run.
- **Judge prompt**: do not edit `qualify/prompt.md` by hand. Change `qualify/spec.json` (or the `qualify` block of the brief) and run `qualify_run.py set-spec`. The four mandatory blocks (size evidence, screening separation, sparse websites, identity language) are enforced in `lib/qualify.py`; they exist because omitting them caused real false negatives in production. Add edge cases as `extra_rules` or extra disqualifies. A locked prompt per idea is saved as `qualify/prompt.locked-<date>.md`; reuse it for a similar idea.
- **Sheet columns**: `lib/schema.py`. **Apollo filters**: `brief.json` (`apollo.base` plus one `filter_sets` entry per segment; keys are Apollo's parameter names, validated by `lib/brief.py`).

## Brief schema (short)
`idea{name,slug,one_liner,memo_source,memo_url,test}`, `icp{summary,hard[],soft[],notes}`, `signals[{name,source_field,op,value,strength}]` (optional, see Phase 4), `pilot{accounts_size,people_size,enabled}` (optional), `apollo{base{...},filter_sets[{label,...}]}` (a set overrides base per key; countries and employee band are mandatory), `people{T1{titles,seniorities},T2{...},include_similar_titles,caps}`, `tier_rules{T1,T2}`, `exclusions{domains,company_keywords,notes}`, `qualify{icp_one_liner,qualifies[],disqualifies[],countries[],lean_direction,thin_evidence_lean}`, `volume{targets,assumptions}`, `decisions[]`. Full example: `examples/brief.example.json`. Industry is deliberately not filtered (labels are self-reported); the AI qualify step decides fit.

## Apollo notes
- Org search `POST /api/v1/mixed_companies/search`: 1 credit per page of up to 100, 500 pages max (50,000 display cap). The client counts first (1 credit each) and shards by location, then employee band, then keywords when a filter exceeds the cap. People search `mixed_people/api_search` costs 0 credits and returns no emails, phones or LinkedIn URLs, an obfuscated last name, and only the company name (not its id or domain). Filtering by `q_organization_domains_list[]` works (checked live). Full name, LinkedIn URL and email need an enrichment call (credits).
- Rate limit on this key: 200 per minute, 6,000 per hour, 50,000 per day. The client reads the response headers, backs off on 429, retries 5xx. No credit caps or approval gates (decision in `PLAN.md` (in docs/)); usage is logged only.
- Search returns companies in two arrays (`organizations` and your CRM `accounts`). Both are read; account records carry the Apollo organization id in `organization_id`.

## How qualification is judged
No model API key exists in the project, so Claude does the judging: `qualify_run.py batches` writes batch files (company facts plus the live homepage title, description and text), Sonnet subagents read them with `prompt.md` and write strict-JSON result files, `qualify_run.py ingest` validates them. To use a paid API later, add a scorer that writes the same result files; nothing else changes. Dead, parked and no-website companies are flagged without the model.

## Tests and troubleshooting
- Offline tests (no network, no credits): `python3 -I engine/tests/run_tests.py` (260 tests: phases 1 to 4)
- Scripts must run with `python3 -I`. If Python hangs on network calls (IPv6), `lib/net.py` already prefers IPv4.
- `state.json is corrupt`: it is set aside as `state.json.bad` and rebuilt from the files. `run is already being worked on`: another run of a script is active; delete `.lock` only if its pid is dead.
- `brief.json changed since accounts were pulled`: re-run `accounts_pull.py` with `--accept-brief-change` (the old pull is archived with a timestamp).
- Re-saving a brief (`brief_save.py`) reuses the newest run folder and sheet for that slug, on any day. `--new-run` starts a fresh dated folder and sheet.
- Changing the qualify spec after `lock` unlocks the prompt (the lock is tied to the exact prompt text): tune again or `lock --force` on the user's say-so.
- Qualify is only `done` (and the run READY) after `finalize`, with every company answered and errors at or below `run.max_error_rate_for_ready`. `batches` and `retry` ingest waiting result files first.
- `bridge refused the secret (403)`: `config.local.json` secret differs from the n8n credential.
- Apollo test pulls (`--limit`) are marked partial and can never produce READY. A brief with `idea.test: true` is never READY either.

## Phase 3 design
### Copy (`/iv-copy`)
- **Library** (`copy-library/`, `bin/copy_library.py build`): read only pull from the BLISS HeyReach workspace (`HEYREACH_API_KEY`). Endpoints, found and tested live: `POST campaign/GetAll`, `GET campaign/GetCampaignSequence?campaignId=` (the sequence as a node tree: nodeType, delay, payload with `messages`, `fallbackMessage`, `conditionalNode` / `unconditionalNode`), `POST stats/GetOverallStats`. One file per campaign plus an index; text is templated; each file lists the rules the old text breaks. `copy-library/README.md` has the patterns and what the numbers do and do not say.
- **Per run** (`lib/copydraft.py`, `bin/copy_run.py`): Claude drafts items as strict JSON (channel li_invite / li_followup / inmail, tier T1 / T2 / all, step, variant V1..V4 or blank, sender founder / associate, subject, body, fallback). Strict ingest: shape errors reject an item, rule errors store it as BLOCKED with the reason; last record per slot wins; nothing is deleted. Slots needed: for every tier with people in "5 Ready: LinkedIn": invite, follow-ups 1 to 3 (`copy.followup_steps`), InMail for tiers in `launch.inmail_tiers` (default T1); each with 3 to 4 variants (`copy.variants_min`, `variants_max`). Tuning is by the user in chat on shown samples, rounds are logged (`tune-log`); `approve` needs a round with 0 corrections on exactly the current items and an explicit note; any later change makes the approval stale. `--simulated` labels rounds and approvals nobody gave (tests): the sheet says `approved (SIMULATED)`, the stage is not done for a real run and the launch refuses it.
- **Voices** (config `copy.voices`): LinkedIn from Jay or Meelan in first person (same text, signed `{MY_FIRST_NAME}`), InMail from a founder's office associate. No default senders. **Frameworks** (config `copy.frameworks`): advisor ask not sales, ask before pitch, permission opener, confirmation CTA, standalone follow-ups with rotating angles (GrowthEngine MIT, ColdIQ ideas only, see NOTICE.md).
- **Rule checker** (`lib/copyrules.py`, pure, tested): port of the coldoutboundskills spam-word-checker lists plus the Blissgrowth rules. Errors (block approval): em or en dash, a link in a first message (invite, follow-up 1, InMail), filler (quick chat, just checking in, checking in, touching base, quick call ...), an internal idea name (config `copy.internal_idea_names`, the run's idea name, any `Bliss<Word>` except Blissgrowth), Blissgrowth spelled differently, a time commitment (30 min, a brief call, an hour), pay numbers, funding amounts, banned phrases, promotional or phishing wording, silence-based closeouts, multiple exclamation marks, unknown placeholders, a missing fallback, length limits (invite 300, InMail 1,900, subject 200). Warnings: COS flagged single words (get, new, now ...; on email they would be errors), sales tone, ALL CAPS, no question, word count outside `copy.word_targets`, duplicates. Extend the lists in `config.json`.

### Launch (`/iv-launch`, HeyReach only)
- **Safety.** `lib/heyreach.py` has no start, resume, pause or delete method (a test greps the source). After creating a campaign the status is read back and must be DRAFT. Execution is refused for a TEST brief, a simulated copy or launch approval, a plan that changed after approval (hash of names, leads, copy and sequences; `approve` also refuses a `plan.json` that is out of date), no real idea page recorded (unless skipped by the user), or missing sender account ids. The user presses Start in HeyReach.
- **Naming** (config `launch.name_template`): `<prefix> - <Tier> - <Region> - <YYYY-MM-DD>`, InMail campaigns add ` - InMail`. The prefix is the idea page's Campaign prefix (`idea-page.json`, then `idea-page/fields.json`, then the brief) and comes first because the reply agent matches campaigns to ideas by it: node "Match Idea" of "HeyReach Reply Agent | 1. Intake & Draft" drops any campaign whose name does not start with "bliss" (case-insensitive) and picks the HRA Ideas row with the longest matching `campaign_prefix` (checked read only, 2026-10-21). The plan and `idea_page.py set-fields` refuse a prefix that does not start with Bliss (`launch.prefix_must_start_with`). HeyReach's tool docs give a 50 character campaign name limit that existing campaigns exceed; see `launch._max_name_len_note`. Region buckets (`launch.regions`): USA, UK, EU (everything else). Groups: tier x region (`launch.group_by`), one HeyReach list and one connect campaign per group, plus an InMail campaign for tiers in `launch.inmail_tiers`.
- **Sequences** (`lib/launch.py`): connect campaign = CHECK_IS_CONNECTION (already connected: end) then CONNECTION_REQUEST (note variants or blank, withdraw after 14 days) then, when accepted, a like and the follow-ups with waits from `launch.message_delays`; InMail campaign = VIEW_PROFILE then INMAIL. Variants V1..V4 go into each node's `messages` array, HeyReach rotates them. A local validator (`validate_sequence`) enforces HeyReach's documented rules (every path ends with END, conditional END after MESSAGE and INMAIL, 3 hour minimum waits, fallback needed with personalisation, size limits); it passes all 79 real sequences of the workspace and has negative tests.
- **Idempotent.** `launch/launch.json` records list ids, leads added and campaign ids; a list or campaign with the same name that this run did not create is never touched (`--adopt-existing-list` for a list). A failure midway resumes without duplicates.

## Phase 4 design (2026-10-22): signals, pilot gates, employment exclusions
Source: the call findings in `CHANGES-PROPOSAL-2026-10-21.md` (signals beat filters, a wrong ICP is the real pain, review the first 10 to 20 by hand, exclude people not in a job). Review notes and open items: the end of this section and "Known gaps".

### Company signals
- **In the brief** (asked in `/iv-brief`, never decided by Claude): `brief.signals`, a list of `{name, source_field, op, value, strength: hard|soft, note}`. `source_field` is `employees`, an Apollo department key (`engineering`, `operations`, `sales`, `information_technology`, `finance`, `marketing`, `human_resources`, `legal` ... the 19 keys are in `config.json` `signals.known_departments`), or a department plus `_pct` (that department as a percent of employees, for ratios). `op`: lt, lte, gt, gte, eq, ne, between (value `[low, high]`); `<`, `<=`, `>`, `>=`, `=`, `!=`, `min` (gte), `max` (lte) are accepted. Free-text signals (`kind: text`, a `description`: tools used, uses consultancies) are judged by Claude from the website and Apollo's technology list and are always soft (a must-have criterion belongs in `icp.hard` and the qualify spec). `lib/signals.py validate` checks all of it, `brief_save.py` refuses a bad list, `1 Brief` shows it.
- **Data**: Apollo `POST /api/v1/organizations/bulk_enrich` (checked in the docs 2026-10-22: `domains[]` in the query, up to 10 per call, 1 credit per matched company, 0 if not found; the answer has `organizations[]` with `estimated_num_employees`, `departmental_head_count`, `technology_names`, and `missing_records`). `GET /organizations/enrich?domain=` (1 credit) is in the client as a single fallback (`ApolloClient.org_enrich`). Live check on the TEST run: 10 domains, 10 matched, 10 credits (one call, `credits_consumed` read from the answer), `departmental_head_count` lists all 19 departments with zeros for empty ones.
- **Cache and credits**: each domain once. `signals/enrich.jsonl` (run) plus `runs/_cache/signals-cache.jsonl` (shared across runs, `signals.cache_days` 90, switch off with `signals.shared_cache` false); matched and not_found are final, errors are retried. Credits are logged by the client in `apollo-usage.jsonl` and in the Run Log (`signals_run.py run`). `--scope pilot` enriches only the pilot accounts, `--limit N` caps the spend.
- **Sheet**: `employees, engineering, operations, sales, information_technology, finance, marketing, human_resources, signals_status, signals_at` are columns of "2 Accounts" (`lib/schema.py`, after the qualify columns so positional appends on older sheets still line up); departments a signal uses beyond those get `dept_<key>`. Added with `add_columns`, values with `upsert` by org_id.
- **Semantics** (all tested): a missing value is `unknown` and never drops. Apollo lists the departments it found people in, so with a non-empty breakdown a missing department is 0 (`signals.missing_department_is_zero`); an empty breakdown is unknown. `sales_pct` = sales / employees, unknown without an employee estimate.
- **In /iv-qualify**: each batch item carries `signals` (values, each rule with its threshold, strength and result pass/fail/unknown/judge, plus Apollo's technology list). HARD rules are applied before the model at `sample` and `batches`: flag `drop`, qualified false, reason `signal: engineers 2000 > max 200`, batch `auto-signal`, row kept, never sent to a subagent or used as a tuning sample, never overwritten by a late result file. A changed threshold releases the row for re-scoring. The judge prompt gets a "Company signals" section appended AFTER the four mandatory blocks (they are untouched and still enforced); `set-spec` copies the brief's signals into the spec. Qualify refuses to run while numeric-signal data is missing.
- **Stage** `signals` (auto): done when every account with a domain has a final answer; done at once when the brief has no numeric signal.

### Pilot gates
- **Why**: "the first 10, 20 subset needs to be reviewed properly" (call). The engine stops twice for a human: before the full account pull, and before enrichment credits are spent on people.
- **Accounts gate** (`pilot_accounts`, registered before `accounts`): `pilot_run.py accounts start` pulls a pilot of 10 to 20 (config `pilot.accounts_size` 15, or `brief.pilot.accounts_size`; sizes outside 10 to 20 are refused) with the existing limit pull, spread over the filter sets, and marks them `pilot = yes`. `signals_run.py run --scope pilot`, then `/iv-qualify` on the pilot (while the gate is open qualify only touches the pilot accounts). `request-review` finalizes qualify and pushes the columns `pilot`, `pilot_review`, `pilot_note`, `is_seed` to "2 Accounts". The user marks approve or reject (any of the words in `pilot.approve_words` / `reject_words`) in the sheet, or Claude records chat marks with `record`; `pull-review` merges the sheet (a blank cell never erases a chat mark, an invalid word is reported). `summary` shows AI versus human (AI keep + rejected = too loose, AI drop + approved = too strict), min/median/max of each headcount for approved versus rejected, and the rejection notes: Claude explains the patterns and proposes brief, signal or prompt changes, and makes none without the user's yes. `approve --note "<what the user said>"` is the explicit approval (refuses while a row is unmarked); the approval stores a hash of the marks, so changing a mark afterwards re-opens the gate ("stale"). After it `accounts_pull.py` (full pull) is allowed; before it, it refuses.
- **People gate** (`pilot_people`, registered before `enrich_verify`): after `/iv-people` and `/iv-tier`, `pilot_run.py people start` draws 10 to 20 selected people (default 15) spread over companies and keeping the T1/T2 mix (seeded, deterministic, never re-drawn). Same `request-review`, `record`, `pull-review`, `summary`, `approve`, `skip`. The columns `pilot`, `pilot_review`, `pilot_note` are on "4 People"; a blank local mark is never pushed over a mark the user typed. People the user rejects get exclusion `pilot_rejected` (no channel) and are never enriched. `enrich_verify.py enrich` refuses to spend until the gate is approved or skipped (a dry run always works).
- **Human beats AI** (applied in `qualify finalize`): a rejected pilot account becomes flag `drop` (reason "pilot review rejected (note); AI: ..."), an approved one that the AI dropped becomes `keep` (dead or parked sites and errors stay). Rows are never deleted.
- **Seeds**: at approval the approved pilot accounts get `is_seed = yes` ("yes (SIMULATED)" for a simulated approval) in "2 Accounts" and `pilot/seeds.csv`, the input for the later lookalike expansion (CHANGES-PROPOSAL section 3). Seeds exist only while the approval is valid.
- **State machine** (derived from files in `pilot/`, `lib/pilot.py`): not_started, selected, enriched, scored, awaiting_review, reviewed, approved; plus skipped (a recorded user decision), stale, simulated (a simulated approval on a real brief) and legacy (accounts already pulled in full, or enrichment already done, before the gates existed: the gate does not apply, so earlier runs and tests are unaffected; a limit test pull is NOT legacy). `pilot.next_action` tells `/iv-run` what to do: auto (pull, enrich signals, request review), claude (qualify the pilot), user (review, approve). `--simulated` labels marks and approvals nobody gave; it is accepted on TEST briefs only and a real run never treats it as done. Everything is written to the Run Log (`pilot_started`, `review_requested`, `review_recorded`, `review_pulled`, `approved`, `skipped`).
- **Config**: `config.json` `pilot` (enabled, sizes, min and max, the approve and reject words). `brief.pilot.enabled: false` or `pilot.enabled: false` switches the gates off for a run.

### Employment exclusions
- Who: people on garden leave, retired, on a career break, between roles, Open to Work (headline or title), and people whose current role has ended. They get `exclusion_reason` in `people.csv` and "4 People" and NO channel (`channels` empty, so they are on no Ready tab and become `withdrawn` on the next build). Rows are never deleted. Counts by category are in `summary.md` ("Exclusions, employment and pilot rejects") and in the Run Log (event `exclusions`, written by `enrich_verify.py`, `activity_run.py`, `ready_build.py` when the numbers change). People already excluded are not sent to the activity checker or the phone reveal; they keep their cap slot (not refilled).
- Sources (`lib/exclusions.py`, applied inside `people.build_rows`, so they are always recomputed from files): (1) keywords in `config.json` `exclusions.groups` on the Apollo headline, the title and the checker's headline and current_title; (2) Apollo `people/bulk_match` data, now stored in `enrich/enrich.jsonl` as `headline` and a compact `employment_history` [{org, title, current, start, end}] (Apollo entry keys per the docs: `current`, `start_date`, `end_date`, `organization_name`, `title`): `apollo_no_current_position` (entries exist, none current, and every entry has an end date; an undated entry is incomplete data, Phase 4 review) and `apollo_role_ended` (the entry at the target company has an end date and is not current); (3) the LinkedIn activity checker columns `headline, current_title, current_company, jobs_count, current_jobs_count, latest_job_start` (now cached by `activity.collect`; `activity_run.py backfill` fills them for older runs from the checker tab, free): `linkedin_no_current_position` (current_jobs_count 0 while jobs_count above 0). Missing data never excludes.
- Keyword list (configurable): garden_leave, retired, career_break, open_to_work (incl. `#opentowork`), between_roles, seeking, looking_for, former. Phrases match on word boundaries, case-insensitive; `re:` makes a regex. DEVIATION from the literal list in the request: the bare words "seeking", "looking for", "former", "ex-" match working people ("CEO (ex-Google)", "Looking for talented engineers"), so the defaults use narrower phrases ("seeking new ...", "looking for my next role", `former` and `ex-` only at the START of the title or the checker's current_title, never the headline, because "Ex-Google | CEO at Acme" is a working person; "in transition" only at the start or as "currently / executive / professional in transition"; Phase 4 review). Put the bare words into the lists in `config.json` if you want them. Each source and the whole feature can be switched off (`exclusions.enabled`, `use_apollo_employment`, `use_linkedin_checker`).
- Reason format: `category: detail; category: detail`, for example `open_to_work: headline says 'Open to Work'; apollo_no_current_position: Apollo employment history lists 4 jobs and none is current`.
- Limits: Apollo employment data can be stale or incomplete (a person who just changed jobs, or a thin history, can look "not current"); the "Open to Work" banner photo has no text; LinkedIn counts come from the checker's scrape. Live check: on the 15 profiles of the TEST run the checker columns were present and no working person was excluded; no live Apollo person with an ended role was available (the stored enrich records predate the new fields), so the Apollo rules are covered by tests built from the documented shape only.
